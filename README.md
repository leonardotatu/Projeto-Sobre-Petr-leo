## 📌 Project Overview

Petroleum (crude oil) is one of the primary sources of energy and raw materials in the modern world. This repository gathers structured and explanatory content to facilitate understanding of the oil industry and its key processes.

### Topics Covered:
* **Origin and Formation:** How organic matter transforms into hydrocarbons over millions of years.
* **Exploration and Drilling:** Methods for locating and drilling oil wells (onshore and offshore).
* **Refining and Derivatives:** Fractionation processes to produce gasoline, diesel, kerosene, plastics, and other subproducts.
* **Geopolitics and Economy:** The impact of oil barrel prices on the global market and the importance of energy transition.
* **Environmental Impacts:** Challenges related to oil spills, carbon emissions, and sustainable alternatives.
